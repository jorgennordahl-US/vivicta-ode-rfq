Section A · Statement of Intent & Cultural Fit
Why Vivicta
Weight: part of 25% Vested-fit criterion · Status: draft

Statement of intent

Vivicta is not a conventional ERP implementer. We are a company built on the conviction that the technology partnership model between vendor and customer is broken — and that fixing it requires a fundamentally different commercial logic. We have invested years developing our practice because we believe that shared value creation, not billable hours, is the only sustainable foundation for a long-term technology relationship. Ode's RFQ reads like it was written by people who have lived through the same frustrations we set out to solve.

How our culture supports WIIFWe

At Vivicta, What's-In-It-For-We is not a framework we apply to engagements — it is the logic we use to evaluate whether to take on an engagement at all. We decline work where the commercial structure would require us to optimise for our own margin at the expense of the client's outcomes. This is not altruism; it is a business model. We grow when our customers grow. Our delivery leads are evaluated on client capability growth, not on dependency. Our commercial team is measured on renewal driven by demonstrated value, not on upsell volume.

Named executive sponsor

[Name of executive sponsor] will serve as Vivicta's executive sponsor for this engagement. [He/she] will be personally accountable to the Joint Governance Board throughout the contract life — co-owning the Desired Outcomes alongside Ode's executive sponsor. This is not a ceremonial role.

Why Ode, why now

The Norwegian aquaculture industry is at an inflection point. Ode's ambition to reach 110,000 tonnes represents exactly the kind of complex, high-stakes environment where a transactional ERP partner will fail and a Vested partner will thrive. We want to be part of building the platform that takes Ode there — not because it is a large contract, but because it is the kind of challenge that demands, and rewards, a genuine partnership. We are ready to sign the NDA and begin.

Section C · Approach to Desired Outcomes
How Vivicta will move each metric
Weight: 25% of evaluation — most important section · Status: draft

Our approach to this section

Ode has asked us to explain, for each Desired Outcome, how our team will move the metric, the expected trajectory, and the risks. We answer each DO directly. We do not hedge. Where we have genuine uncertainty — specifically around the aquaculture-specific operational systems — we say so and explain how we will resolve it jointly during contracting. Baselines and precise targets will be co-defined with Ode during the Vested contracting phase; the trajectories below reflect our experience from comparable integrated value-chain implementations.

DO1 — One integrated value-chain platform

Measure: # legacy systems retired · % of transactions running in D365 F&O

Our approach is a phased consolidation anchored on a standardised D365 F&O core deployed once and extended — not re-implemented — as Ode adds entities and sites. We will establish a single chart of accounts, a single legal-entity structure aligned with Ode Land / Ode Hav / Ode Processing & Sales, and a master data governance model on day one of go-live. Visma ERP is the priority retirement: we target 100% of financial transactions in D365 F&O within 12 months of go-live. Operational systems (Mercatus, Innova, Maritech) are integrated — not replaced — in the first phase, with replacement evaluated jointly in the continuous improvement backlog.

⚑ Key risk: Data quality in Visma is unknown until we run extraction. We will run a data quality sprint in week 1 of mobilisation and agree acceptance criteria before migration begins.

DO2 — Reliable, real-time business truth

Measure: Days to close month-end · data quality / reconciliation defects

Today's fragmented landscape forces manual reconciliation between Visma, Maritech, and operational systems — making month-end slow and error-prone. Our target is a hard-close cycle of 3 working days within 6 months of go-live, moving to 2 days by end of year 1. We achieve this by eliminating manual journal entry through automated posting from integrated operational systems, by deploying D365's financial period-close workspace as the single control surface, and by connecting Microsoft Fabric from day one so that operational and financial data is queryable in real time. The Power App-driven production cost closure model described in Ode's companion document (ODE-PRODORDER-2026) is directly supported by our process manufacturing practice and will be a design priority in phase 1.

⚑ Key risk: Real-time truth depends on integration quality from Mercatus Farmer and Innova. We will co-own integration acceptance criteria with Ode's operations team and will not declare go-live until data reconciliation defects are below the jointly agreed threshold.

DO3 — Lower total cost to operate

Measure: Total ERP TCO vs. 2026 baseline (NOK/year)

TCO reduction comes from three levers: (1) retiring the on-premise Visma infrastructure and its associated support and upgrade costs; (2) consolidating the fragmented HR and time-registration landscape into D365 HR and integrated payroll, reducing manual reconciliation effort; and (3) operating D365 as a cloud SaaS with a standardised application management service that scales in cost as Ode's user base grows, not as a function of bespoke customisations. Our open-book commercial model means Ode will see exactly where money is spent throughout the contract. We commit to establishing a baseline TCO measurement methodology jointly in month 1 and to reporting against it at every Outcomes & Value Board.

⚑ Key risk: TCO reduction in year 1 may be offset by parallel-run costs during migration. We will model this explicitly in Section J so Ode has a realistic net TCO trajectory rather than a headline number.

DO4 — Faster time-to-value for change

Measure: Lead time for new entity / site / product onboarding

Ode's growth plan toward 110,000 tonnes requires the platform to absorb new sea sites, new legal entities, and new product lines without re-implementation. We design for this from day one by using D365's standard multi-entity and multi-site architecture, by avoiding customisations that would create upgrade friction, and by building a Vivicta-owned configuration accelerator specific to Ode's value chain. Our target is that a new Norwegian legal entity or production site can be onboarded in D365 within 6 weeks from business decision to go-live. We will agree a formal onboarding runbook with Ode during contracting and test it on the first new site as a proof point.

⚑ Key risk: Speed of onboarding depends on Ode's internal capacity to participate in configuration and testing. We will design the knowledge transfer plan (DO5) specifically to build this internal muscle.

DO5 — User adoption and capability

Measure: Active user % per process · certified internal super-users

User adoption is not a training event — it is a design principle. We embed Ode's super-users in the implementation team from day one: they design the processes alongside us, own the test scripts, and deliver training to their colleagues. Our target is that Ode has a minimum of 8 certified D365 super-users covering finance, supply chain, production, and HR before go-live, and that active user rate exceeds 85% within 3 months of go-live. We will measure adoption by process area monthly and report it at the Delivery / Run Forum. Vivicta's knowledge transfer plan is a contractual deliverable — and DO5 performance is directly tied to our outcome bonus (Section E).

⚑ Key risk: With 220+ employees across multiple sites, adoption requires sustained change management effort. We will propose a dedicated change lead embedded in the Ode team for the first 12 months.

DO6 — Audit-ready, compliant, secure

Measure: Audit findings · critical security incidents

D365 F&O on Microsoft Azure is architected for Norwegian and EU regulatory compliance from the ground up. We will configure Norwegian tax (MVA), bookkeeping act compliance, and Altinn reporting as standard. Food traceability — batch tracking from hatchery through grow-out to processed product — will be implemented using D365's standard batch and serial number tracking, extended for Ode's biological inventory model. Information security is governed through Microsoft Entra ID, conditional access, and role-based access control with least-privilege principles. Our target is zero critical security incidents and zero material audit findings attributable to the ERP platform. Compliance configuration is documented and version-controlled so that every change is auditable.

⚑ Key risk: CSRD and Norwegian sustainability reporting requirements are evolving. We include a regulatory monitoring commitment in the AMS scope so Ode is never caught by a compliance change without warning.

DO7 — Platform for AI and data

Measure: # productive AI agents in production · estimated annual cost avoidance (NOK)

As a Microsoft AI Inner Circle partner, Vivicta deploys AI from day one — not as a future roadmap item. D365 F&O connects to Microsoft Fabric at go-live, with all operational data from Mercatus, Innova, and Maritech flowing into a unified lakehouse. Vivicta has developed a library of aquaculture-specific AI agent patterns for Norwegian fish farming operations, drawing on deep knowledge of the biological, financial, and regulatory complexity of the sector.

We propose deploying four agents in VCIM Phase 1 and three additional agents in Phase 2. Our experience with comparable Norwegian aquaculture finance and operations environments puts the quantified benefit of this portfolio at approximately NOK 2–3M in annual cost avoidance — driven by month-end automation (120+ hours/year), feed cost anomaly detection (1–2% cost reduction), harvest forecast accuracy improvement, and grunnrenteskatt compliance automation. Every agent has its own A3 business case and agreed KPIs before development begins.

We target a minimum of 4 productive AI agents in production by end of VCIM Phase 1, with the full portfolio of 7 operational by Phase 2. The AI backlog is jointly owned at every Outcomes & Value Board — never empty, never unaccountable.

⚑ Key risk: AI agents require clean, structured data — which depends on DO1 and DO2 first. We sequence deliberately: Fabric data foundation months 1–6, first agent in production month 9, full portfolio by VCIM Phase 2 trigger.

Section D · Implementation & Run Model
How we deliver and operate
Weight: supports 25% Outcomes + 15% D365 capability criteria · Status: draft

Delivery philosophy

Vivicta delivers D365 F&O using an agile, outcome-driven methodology — not a waterfall project plan. We work in two-week sprints with a shared backlog owned jointly by Ode and Vivicta. Every sprint produces working, tested functionality in a live D365 environment. There are no "big bang" go-lives: we phase by value chain segment, starting with the financial backbone and growing into operational integration, so Ode starts realising value within 3–4 months of mobilisation, not at the end of an 18-month project. The engagement has three phases: Foundation, Value Chain Extension, and Continuous Improvement. Phases 1 and 2 are time-boxed; Phase 3 is the ongoing Vested partnership with no defined end date.

Phase 1 — Foundation (months 1–9): financial backbone & core integrations

Scope: three-entity legal structure (Ode Land, Ode Hav, Ode Processing & Sales AS), General Ledger, AP/AR, Fixed Assets, Bank, Tax, Consolidation, Procurement & Sourcing, Inventory, Cost Accounting, Power BI/Fabric baseline, Visma ERP data migration, Entra ID security model, and integration to Mercatus Farmer and Innova. Go-live target: month 9 from project start (mid-May 2027 if mobilisation begins mid-August 2026). Ode's finance team runs month-end in D365 for the first time at go-live. Visma ERP is switched off within 30 days of go-live.

Phase 2 — Value chain extension (months 10–18): production, sales & HR

Scope: Production control / process manufacturing for harvesting and Vartdal processing, the Power App period-close frontend (ODE-PRODORDER-2026), Sales & Marketing, Maritech ERP-ANY API integration, HR core, integration to Visma Payroll and Tidsbanken replacement, and first Copilot Studio agent in production. By end of phase 2, all seven value chain stages — broodstock, hatchery, grow-out, fish health, harvesting, processing, sales — are connected through a single D365 backbone.

Phase 3 — Continuous improvement (month 19+): the Vested partnership

Scope: Application management services (AMS), 2nd/3rd line support, Microsoft release management (D365 updates two times per year), continuous improvement backlog, new site and entity onboarding, AI/data use case delivery, and annual Exit & Continuity Review. There is no re-implementation. The platform grows with Ode. The improvement backlog is jointly owned and prioritised at every Outcomes & Value Board.

Team structure

The Vivicta core team for Ode will consist of: a dedicated Delivery Lead (onshore Norway, fluent in Norwegian business and regulatory context), a Solution Architect (D365 F&O, process manufacturing specialism), a Finance Functional Lead, a Supply Chain & Production Functional Lead, an Integration Architect (Azure Integration Services, Mercatus API, Maritech ERP-ANY), a Data & AI Lead (Fabric, Copilot Studio), a Change & Adoption Lead, and a Test Lead. Development resource will be provided from Vivicta's nearshore centres in the Baltics and Czech Republic for cost efficiency, with all client-facing roles onshore in Norway or Sweden. The team is sized for phase 1; it scales up for phase 2 and right-sizes to a lean AMS team in phase 3.

The exit management clause (Section F) guarantees that Ode can transition to a different partner or insource at any time without losing access to any of this material.

How the model scales to 110,000 tonnes

Ode's growth plan requires the ERP to onboard new sea sites, new land-based facilities, and eventually new species or geographies. Vivicta's delivery model is designed for exactly this: the D365 core is implemented once with multi-entity and multi-site architecture; new sites are onboarded using the configuration accelerator without re-implementation; the nearshore team provides elastic capacity for onboarding sprints without Ode bearing the cost of a permanently oversized team. We commit to a maximum 6-week onboarding lead time for new Norwegian entities from month 18 onwards, reducing to 4 weeks by year 3 as the runbook matures.

The long-term vision: towards a transaction-free operating model

Vivicta's ambition for this partnership goes beyond delivering and running a D365 platform. We want to lead Ode towards a fundamentally different way of operating ERP — one where routine transactions, maintenance decisions, and compliance checks are increasingly handled by AI agents, and where Vivicta's human team focuses almost entirely on business-driven value creation.

We call this the Vivicta Continuous Improvement Model (VCIM) — a structured, three-layer operating model that governs how the partnership evolves from implementation through to a mature, AI-driven state. It is not a future concept. It is the operating model we will begin building from day one, with each phase of the implementation designed to enable the next layer of the model.

Section E · Pricing Model
Two tracks. One principle: we earn when you win.
Weight: part of 15% TCO + 25% Vested-fit · Status: draft — Elise to confirm with leadership before submission

The governing principle

Vivicta's commercial model is built on a single principle: we recover our costs through a transparent baseline fee, and we earn our profit only when Ode realises measurable business value. There are no hourly rates for business-driven development. There are no change request charges within agreed scope. Our margin is not hidden in the baseline — it is earned in arrears, in proportion to the gains we help create.

The model has two clearly separated tracks: Management & Operations (forvaltning) and Business-Driven Development (forretningsdrevet utvikling). Each track has its own commercial logic, its own governance, and its own accountability structure. Together they form a single, coherent partnership model that aligns every incentive Vivicta has with Ode's business outcomes.

Track 1 — Management & Operations (forvaltning)

This track covers everything required to keep the platform running, current, and compliant — regardless of whether it creates incremental business value. It includes application management services (AMS), 2nd/3rd line support, Microsoft release management (two major D365 updates per year), security patching, performance monitoring, and standard configuration changes that do not constitute new business capability.

Commercial structure: cost recovery only, zero margin. Ode pays Vivicta's fully loaded cost — people, tooling, infrastructure — with open-book transparency. Vivicta earns no profit on this track. The monthly cost breakdown is published in a shared open-book dashboard accessible to both parties at all times.

This track has no business case requirement. It runs continuously from go-live and is governed through the Delivery & Run Forum (Section F).

Track 2 — Business-Driven Development (forretningsdrevet utvikling)

This track covers all initiatives that create new business capability with a quantifiable expected value: new modules, AI agents, process redesigns, integrations that enable new revenue or cost reduction, and any development project that goes beyond keeping the lights on. Every initiative on this track — without exception — requires its own signed business case before work begins.

Commercial structure: cost recovery + 30% of identified value as profit, paid in three tranches.

20%
Value mapped
Business case signed by joint Steering Committee. Gains identified, quantified, and measurement methodology agreed by both parties.
40%
Technology delivered
Solution live, integrations operational, user adoption above agreed threshold. Ode's operations running on the new capability.
40%
Gains realised
Measured gains confirmed in Ode's P&L. This tranche is prorated to actual realisation — if 80% of the committed gain is realised, Vivicta receives 80% of tranche 3. Gains not realised due to factors within Vivicta's control reduce the tranche accordingly.

The 40/40 split between the delivery and realisation tranches reflects a deliberate balance: Vivicta carries meaningful financial risk on gain realisation, while retaining enough certainty on delivery milestones to maintain commercial sustainability. 40% of our profit on every development project is held at risk until Ode confirms the value is real.

The gain confirmation letter (gevinstbrev)

Every business-driven development project closes with a formal Gain Confirmation Letter — a document signed by Ode's authorised representative confirming the gains achieved, the measurement methodology used, and the percentage of the committed target realised. This letter is the trigger for tranche 3 payment.

The Gain Confirmation Letter is not a Vivicta document presented to Ode for signature. It is Ode's document — drafted by Ode's own team, based on the jointly agreed measurement methodology from the business case. Vivicta provides the data and analysis; Ode owns the conclusion. This structure ensures that gain realisation is something Ode actively certifies, not something Vivicta claims.

If Ode cannot or will not issue a Gain Confirmation Letter, tranche 3 is not payable. This creates a powerful incentive on both sides: Vivicta to deliver real, measurable value; Ode to commit to the organisational changes required to realise it.

The pre-project — joint value identification

Track 1 / Track 2 boundary — the decision rule

The most important governance decision in this model is determining whether a piece of work is Track 1 (forvaltning, cost recovery) or Track 2 (forretningsdrevet utvikling, business case required). The decision rule is simple and contractually defined:

Track 1 if the work is required to maintain the existing platform at its current functional level — including Microsoft-mandated updates, security patches, bug fixes, and configuration changes that restore rather than extend capability.

Track 2 if the work creates new or materially improved business capability that did not exist before — regardless of whether it is technically a "development" or a "configuration" task.

Boundary disputes are resolved at the Delivery & Run Forum within 5 business days. If unresolved, escalation goes to the Outcomes & Value Board. Neither party can unilaterally classify work. The Delivery Lead (Vivicta) and Project Owner (Ode) must both agree on classification before work begins.

Escalation

Escalation is explicitly not a sign of failure — it is a designed mechanism to resolve genuine disagreements quickly. The escalation ladder has three levels: (1) Delivery Lead + Project Owner within 5 days; (2) Outcomes & Value Board within 15 days; (3) Joint Steering Committee within 30 days. If the Joint Steering Committee cannot resolve the matter, both parties agree to a structured mediation process before any legal action — with a jointly appointed mediator from a pre-agreed panel.

Exit & continuity

Ode can exit this contract at any time with 6 months' written notice, without penalty beyond the notice period and any Track 2 tranches already triggered. On exit, Vivicta is contractually required to: transfer all configuration, code, and documentation to Ode or a nominated successor partner within the notice period; provide 40 hours of transition support per month during the notice period at cost; and release all access to Ode's Azure tenant, DevOps environment, and data assets within 5 business days of contract end. There is no lock-in. Ode owns everything.

Deployment architecture

D365 Finance & Supply Chain Management will be deployed on Microsoft Azure, hosted in the Norway East and Norway West regions. All Ode data — financial, operational, and personal — remains within Norway and the EU/EEA at all times. There is no data residency exception. Identity and access

All access to Ode's D365 environment is governed through Microsoft Entra ID with conditional access policies enforcing multi-factor authentication, device compliance, and location-based access controls. Role-based access control (RBAC) is implemented on least-privilege principles — every user and every Vivicta consultant has access only to the data and functions required for their role. Privileged access (system administration, configuration changes) requires just-in-time elevation through Entra Privileged Identity Management with a full audit trail. No standing privileged access is permitted.

Separation of duties is enforced in D365 for all financial workflows — no single user can both create and approve a payment, journal entry, or purchase order above defined thresholds. The separation of duties matrix will be designed jointly with Ode's CFO and internal audit function during phase 1.

Norwegian regulatory compliance

D365 will be configured to meet all applicable Norwegian regulatory requirements from go-live: MVA (Norwegian VAT), the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act (Bokføringsloven), Altinn electronic reporting, SAF-T financial reporting, and payroll tax reporting. Vivicta maintains a dedicated Norwegian compliance practice and will ensure that every Microsoft release update is assessed for regulatory impact before deployment — with no compliance gap permitted between a regulatory effective date and D365 configuration.

Food safety and traceability obligations under Norwegian and EU regulations (including the EU General Food Law) will be addressed through D365's standard batch and serial number tracking, extended for Ode's aquaculture value chain. Batch traceability from broodstock through hatchery, grow-out, harvesting, and processing to final customer will be a phase 1 design requirement — not a phase 2 afterthought.

CSRD and sustainability reporting

Ode will be subject to CSRD reporting obligations as the regulatory scope expands to Norwegian mid-sized companies.
